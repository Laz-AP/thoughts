*Building story 02 · Video essay and sketches*

Standing at 53 West Jackson Boulevard, the Monadnock Building is sixteen stories and 215 feet of load-bearing brick. When it was completed it was the tallest load-bearing masonry commercial building in the world. Its walls are about six feet thick at the base and taper to eighteen inches at the top.

## Two halves, two architects

Burnham & Root designed the north half, begun in 1891. Holabird & Roche designed the south half, built in 1892 and 1893, and its section is steel-framed. Root's wind bracing was among the first portal systems used in the United States.

## Landmark and restoration

It was one of the first five Chicago Architectural Landmarks, named in 1958, and was designated a Chicago Landmark in 1973. A restoration that ran from 1979 to 1992 returned it to its original condition.
